# Artwork for HAMID — Nur mal kurz.

Two original illustrations were generated using the **built-in image_gen tool**. The supplied family photo was used solely as an identity reference. The source photograph is not included in this repository. Final generated assets are saved at:

- `public/art/hamid-cover.png`: cinematic title artwork.
- `public/art/hamid-avatar.png`: circular in-game portrait.

The playable apartment, articulated protagonist, furniture, cats, lighting, animation, and particles are original runtime Three.js geometry in `src/world.ts`. They are gameplay objects, not pre-rendered images. Lucide provides interface icons. The tiny favicon is an original typographic H.

## Exact cover prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one finished 16:9 wide cinematic illustration for a premium humorous household game start screen.
Input image 1: person identity reference only. Preserve the recognizable likeness, bald head with short gray side hair, expressive kind eyes, rectangular dark glasses, broad warm smile and facial proportions of the older man in the reference. Translate him into an affectionate, appealing polished 3D caricature.
Primary request: Hamid stands center-right in his cozy apartment, smiling confidently and holding a screwdriver upright at chest level. He wears a textured blue knit sweater over a white collared shirt and dark trousers. Two large pudgy cats, one ginger and one black-and-white tuxedo, sit prominently in the foreground with playful expressive faces. A safe balcony ladder and cozy apartment with a retro mainframe workstation are visible in the background. A magenta telephone is ringing in the background, expressed through a slightly tilted handset and tiny subtle motion cues without any letters.
Style/medium: high quality finished stylized 3D animated feature key art, rich rounded forms, finely crafted materials, charming affectionate domestic comedy, sophisticated cinematic production value.
Composition/framing: landscape 16:9, full-screen game artwork, keep Hamid and all busy focal detail in the right two thirds. Reserve the entire left third as darker low-detail teal interior negative space suitable for later HTML title typography. Show enough of Hamid for his sweater and screwdriver to read immediately. Cats anchor the lower right and lower center.
Lighting/mood: warm golden hour sunlight from the apartment balcony, teal ambient shadows, soft cinematic depth, warm rim light, optimistic humor.
Materials/textures: tactile blue wool knit, soft well-groomed cat fur, warm wood and subtle retro computing details.
Constraints: exactly one finished illustration, no variants, no collage, no text, no logo, no watermark, no ethnic stereotypes, no danger or injuries, anatomically coherent hands. Keep the recognizable reference likeness.
```

## Exact portrait prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one finished square HUD avatar portrait for a premium humorous household game.
Input image 1: person identity reference only. Preserve the recognizable likeness, bald head with short gray side hair, expressive kind eyes, rectangular dark glasses, broad warm smile and facial proportions of the older man in the reference. Translate him into an affectionate, appealing polished 3D caricature.
Primary request: expressive smiling and quietly confident portrait of Hamid from the chest up, wearing a tactile blue knit sweater over a crisp white collared shirt. Face and glasses clearly visible, eyes looking towards the viewer, warm familiar personality.
Style/medium: high quality finished stylized 3D animated feature character portrait, rich rounded forms, finely crafted materials, affectionate domestic comedy, sophisticated production value.
Composition/framing: square 1:1, centered chest-up portrait, keep the entire head comfortably inside the central circular safe area with generous clean space around it so it works in a circular HUD crop.
Scene/backdrop: clean dark teal studio background with a subtle depth gradient and no objects.
Lighting/mood: flattering soft warm key light, cool teal fill, warm studio rimlight, charming and reassuring.
Constraints: exactly one finished illustration, no variants, no collage, no text, no logo, no watermark, no props, no ethnic stereotypes. Keep the recognizable reference likeness and the blue sweater, white collar and rectangular dark glasses.
```

Both outputs were visually inspected. The cover's ladder is not prominent; the playable balcony includes the actual ladder station. No generated variations or other external artwork are used.
